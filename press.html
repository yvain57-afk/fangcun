What it is

Little Exhale is a native iPhone app for gentle breathing and body practices. A small cat and dog keep you company, with movements that follow the practice. English guidance, clear visual cues and skippable introductions help you start at your own pace. Pause or finish whenever you need.

Ten ways to take a small pause

A two-part sigh, longer-out-breath practice, balanced breathing, box breathing, gentle belly breathing, alternate-nostril breathing, meditation, a resting body scan (NSDR), pelvic floor practice, and progressive muscle relaxation.

A calmer approach to daily care

Optional feeling and drink records, practice history and small acts of care sit in Daily. No account is required. Core records stay on your devices; Apple Health is optional. Bundled audio works offline. Appearance, larger text and reduced-motion options help you make the space your own.

For editors and reviewers

Try a short breathing practice, skip its introduction, and compare voice guidance with quiet visual guidance. The companions do not require feeding or virtual-pet chores. We welcome independent feedback; no positive review is expected.

These are general wellbeing practices. We do not claim app-specific clinical validation or treatment of a medical condition.
